Closed form

The $n$-th Fibonacci number has a closed form (Binet’s formula):

$$F_n = \frac{\varphi^n - \psi^n}{\sqrt{5}}, \quad \varphi = \frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}.$$

So you can skip the loop — at the cost of floating-point precision.
